I build not separate scripts but a living system around AI. It has a director that runs a dozen working agent sessions, a memory that loses nothing, a router that picks the model and thinking depth for every step, and my own servers where all of it runs unattended. Everything shown here runs in production, across three machines and a phone. I take a task end to end, from research and architecture to tests, deployment and post-launch monitoring. For me 'done' means run, deliberately broken and shown in a log. An idea that failed an honest check gets closed, not fitted to the result.
